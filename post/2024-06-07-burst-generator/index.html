Burst Generator

Thu, Jun 6, 2024

Sometimes you just want burst quick sequence of pulses to fire every once in a while. This is where a burst generator can come in handy.

This build is based on Ken Stone’s “Cat Girl Synth” Burst Generator as documented in the sdiy site.
