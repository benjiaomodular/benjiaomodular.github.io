Spring Reverb

Thu, Jun 27, 2024
Draft +/-12v analog pcb

I used a power amp module to drive a DC motor.

I used this tutorial by The Electronic Peasant on how to turn a DC motor into a rotary transtucer.

The vibrations of the springs are picked up using a hand formed bridge with a piezo transducer, similar to an acoustic guitar pickup.

I used this circuit to amplify the piezo output.
